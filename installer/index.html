CGO600 Bridge Installer

Turn an ESP32 into a bridge that reads your Tenways CGO600 over Bluetooth and brings battery, range, trips and the light into Home Assistant. No tools to install, just your browser.

  • An ESP32 board; the ESP32-WROOM-32U with an external antenna gives the best range
  • A USB data cable (not a charge-only cable)
  • Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Web Serial)
Recommended for Home Assistant

ESPHome

  • Set up WiFi right here after flashing
  • Discovered automatically by Home Assistant
  • Adopt and customise it in the ESPHome add-on
Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Web Serial needs a secure (https) connection.
Standalone

Standalone firmware

  • Built-in web dashboard you can install as an app
  • Local ride history inferred from check-ins
  • Home Assistant through MQTT discovery
  • WiFi set up through the "CGO600-Setup" hotspot
Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Web Serial needs a secure (https) connection.

After installing

  1. ESPHome: enter your WiFi details when the installer asks. Home Assistant then shows the bridge under Settings → Devices & services.
  2. Standalone: connect your phone to the CGO600-Setup hotspot and choose your WiFi. Then open http://cgo600.local to add your MQTT broker.
  3. Switch the bike on near the bridge; it connects automatically. No pairing or MAC address is needed.

A new install erases the ESP32, including any ride history stored by the standalone firmware. Later updates are installed over WiFi.
