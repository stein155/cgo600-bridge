CGO600 Bridge Installer

Turn an ESP32 into a bridge that reads your Tenways CGO600 over Bluetooth and brings battery, range, trips and the light into Home Assistant. No tools to install, just your browser.

  • An ESP32 board; the ESP32-WROOM-32U with an external antenna gives the best range
  • A USB data cable (not a charge-only cable)
  • Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Web Serial)
  • Upgrading from the old standalone firmware? Export your rides first via http://<bridge>/api/rides, then use Import rides in the new dashboard.

What you get

  • A dashboard on the bridge itself: live battery, range and light, plus your ride history
  • Battery, range, trips, light and more in Home Assistant, discovered automatically
  • Rides inferred from departure and arrival, also sent to Home Assistant as events
  • A Bluetooth proxy for other devices near your bike shed
  • Built on ESPHome: updates over WiFi, and adopt it in the ESPHome add-on to customise
Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Web Serial needs a secure (https) connection.

After installing

  1. Enter your WiFi details when the installer asks for them. If you skip this step, connect to the CGO600 Bridge hotspot instead.
  2. Click Visit device to open the bridge dashboard, and bookmark it. The address is also shown under Settings → System.
  3. Add the bridge in Home Assistant under Settings → Devices & services; it is usually discovered automatically.
  4. Switch the bike on near the bridge. It connects automatically; no pairing or MAC address is needed.

A new install erases the ESP32. Later updates are installed over WiFi, and the ride history is kept.
